About

Physician and psychiatric researcher. I study how childhood adversity shapes the way the brain and the body answer stress — with neuroimaging, magnetic resonance spectroscopy and machine learning.

MD thesis at Friedrich Schiller University Jena with Dr Lejla Colic and Prof Dr Martin Walter. Approbation as a physician, 2025.

Research focus

  • Stress responsivity

  • Neuroimaging · fMRI & MRS
  • Machine learning & NLP
