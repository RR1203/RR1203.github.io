About

I am a physician and psychiatric researcher interested in childhood adversity, stress responsivity, depression, and translational neuroscience.

Research

My research focuses on understanding the biological and psychological mechanisms underlying psychiatric disorders, with particular interests in stress, adversity, neuroimaging, and psychiatric heterogeneity.

01

Childhood adversity & stress

How early-life adversity shapes stress responsivity, and why some people prove resilient while others do not.

02

Depression & heterogeneity

Why the same diagnosis looks so different from person to person, and what that means for treatment and prognosis.

03

Translational neuroscience

Connecting neuroimaging and biological markers with the questions that matter in day-to-day clinical psychiatry.
