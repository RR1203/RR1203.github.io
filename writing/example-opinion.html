An opinion piece, in eight hundred words

A short argument about clinical practice, research culture or the way psychiatry talks about itself.

An opinion piece works well at six hundred to nine hundred words: one claim, two or three reasons, and a close that does not hedge everything away again.

The second paragraph is where the argument earns its keep. Evidence, a case, a number, or a plain observation from the clinic.
